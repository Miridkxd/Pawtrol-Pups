Before you report

Read this carefully before filing an animal abuse report online.

📋 Requirements

  • Be of legal age (18+) or have a parent/guardian present.
  • Have a valid national ID (DNI) or passport on hand.
  • Provide real name and contact info.
  • Have evidence if possible: photos, videos, witnesses.
  • Describe facts clearly and in order.

⚖️ Implications

  • False reports are a criminal offense.
  • The report enters an official legal process.
  • You may be contacted by authorities.
  • Penalties under Law 14.346.

🔄 Process

  1. Submit the official form.
  2. Authorities receive it.
  3. Case is reviewed.
  4. Investigation may start.
  5. Track with report number.

Pawtrol Pups does not intervene in legal processes. We only provide access to official tools.
